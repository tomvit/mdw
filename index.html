Cloud Technologies 1

The Cloud Technologies 1 is a post-graduate university course which provides underlying architectural and theoretical concepts about enterprise and software architecture, service-oriented and microservice architectures, cloud architectures and cloud-native technologies. The course covers cloud infrastructure and service models, containers, Kubernetes, communication protocols such as HTTP/2, and techniques for achieving high availability and performance. In my lectures, I share example problems from large-scale integration and cloud infrastructure domains and point out technological and architectural limitations with possibilities to control and overcome them.
